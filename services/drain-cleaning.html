Why Knoxville Drains Clog

Knoxville's older neighborhoods — Sequoyah Hills, Fourth & Gill, and Old North Knoxville — still run on aging cast-iron and clay sewer laterals, and the city's mature tree canopy sends roots straight into them. Add East Tennessee's freeze-thaw winters and ridge-and-valley terrain, and pipes separate at the joints. That's why a quick snake sometimes fails: the real fix starts with a camera.
