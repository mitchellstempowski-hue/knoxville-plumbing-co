Local know-how

Knoxville Water Heaters vs. Hard Water

Much of the Knoxville metro pulls water with meaningful mineral content, and that sediment settles in the bottom of every tank-style heater — cutting efficiency, causing that rumbling "kettling" noise, and rotting tanks from the inside years early. Most Knoxville tanks last 8–12 years with basic maintenance, less without it. If your heater is past 10 and acting up, replacement usually beats another repair. Our pros will give you the honest math for your specific unit.
