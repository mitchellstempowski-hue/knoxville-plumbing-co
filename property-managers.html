How it works

Set It Up Once. We Handle the Requests.

Managing rentals means plumbing problems are a when, not an if. Here's the whole system — it takes one lease-packet update to put in place.

One form for every tenant

Put our quote form link in your lease packet as the plumbing contact, or submit requests yourself — either way, every request lands in the same place and a real person picks it up.

Tenant gets matched

Your tenant is matched with one local pro who coordinates scheduling and access with them directly — and calls them back fast. No phone tag with you stuck in the middle.

You approve the price

Nothing gets fixed on your dime without your say-so. The pro quotes a flat price, you approve it, then the work starts. The invoice you pay is the price you approved.

What it costs you

Nothing. That's the Whole Pitch.

The referral is free for you and free for your tenants. Knoxville Plumbing Co is a matching service, not a management company: there's no contract, no monthly fee, and no markup on the pro's work. You pay only the pro's invoice, at the flat price you approved before work started — the same price you'd negotiate calling around yourself, minus the afternoon spent calling around.

Because the pros in our network know the job details and the property before they roll, quotes come back itemized and flat — drain clearing, water heater swaps, leak repairs, and the middle-of-the-night emergencies alike. If a matched pro ever stops performing, tell us and we'll match the unit with a different pro, free.
